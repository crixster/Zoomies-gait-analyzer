A tempo prescription uses four numbers (e.g., 3-0-1-0) to tell you how many seconds to spend on each part of the movement:

  • 1st Digit (Eccentric): The time in seconds to lower the weight.
  • 2nd Digit (Bottom Pause): The time in seconds to pause at the bottom of the lift.
  • 3rd Digit (Concentric): The time in seconds to lift or push the weight back up. (An X means lift as fast and explosively as possible).
  • 4th Digit (Top Pause): The time in seconds to pause at the top of the lift.
